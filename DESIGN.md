# Design System: AkAI

**Creative North Star: "The Shopfront"** — in Addis, one painted board carries Amharic and English at equal weight. The landing page is that board.

- Ground: enamel teal with vertical sheet-metal ribs; saw-tooth saffron edge at top and bottom.
- Signs: chalk-white enamel frame, 3px ink border, rivets in the corners, painted keyline inside a flat vermilion / saffron / sky / enamel panel, hard flat offset shadow (no blur, no glow).
- Buttons are painted tabs: ink border, hard 5px shadow that presses in on active.
- Both scripts at identical weight; Big Shoulders and heavy Noto Sans Ethiopic read as one hand.
- One authored motion: the round decal landing on the sign pair (translation).
- Do not reintroduce: gradients, glass, glow, the flag-colour stamp/airmail treatment.

App icon: a painted keycap tilted -6° on ribbed teal, with the Ge'ez አ (first fidel) in vermilion and a small Latin A as its key legend.
